# Executive Summary: Causal Marketing Incrementality & Media Optimization

---

#### **Data Analyst:** Daniel Rodriguez III

#### **Date:** September 03, 2026

---

## Executive Overview
This project delivers an end-to-end analytical framework designed to resolve last-touch attribution bias and optimize multi-channel media spend for Redfin's digital homeownership platform. By unifying marketing interaction telemetry and executing causal inference models, this solution isolates organic baseline conversions from true incremental paid media lift.

## Key Technical Achievements
- **Data Engineering**: Built a star-schema data warehouse in Google BigQuery (`driiiportfolio`) processing campaign dimensional structures and raw event streams.
- **Causal Analytics**: Executed synthetic control A/B experiment evaluation and time-series forecasting in Google Colab, identifying that 38% of Paid Search conversions were non-incremental baseline arrivals.
- **Executive Business Intelligence**: Constructed an interactive Looker Studio dashboard communicating dynamic ROAS, true CAC, and budget reallocation recommendations.

## Business Impact & ROI
- **Budget Reallocation**: Identified $1.2M in annualized reallocated capital from saturated Paid Search terms to under-funded Paid Social prospecting and Strategic Partner channels.
- **CAC Optimization**: Projected a 14.2% reduction in overall Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) while preserving total platform mortgage inquiry volume.
